Enclosed is a publication by Chloe Chow documenting a collection of stripes found throughout Manhattan, primarily in Chelsea. Inspired by Michel Foucault’s writing on the Panopticon and his idea of “power of mind over mind,” these everyday lines: gates, fences, vents, and barriers, become visual symbols of control, restriction, and surveillance.

Enclosed looks at how surveillance can shape our behavior even when we are not actively aware of being watched. By mapping the exact locations, times, and paths behind each photograph, this digital archive turns my movement through the city into something that can be traced and observed. The viewer takes on the role of the watcher, reconstructing my behavior through the information I leave behind.

Enclosed, Accordion Book, Chloe Chow, 2026
